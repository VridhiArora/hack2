Health Risks Based on BMI Range

Mild Underweight (BMI 17 - 18.4)
  • Nutritional deficiencies
  • Weakened immune system
  • Fatigue and low energy
  • Possible hormonal imbalances
Moderate Underweight (BMI 16 - 16.9)
  • Increased nutritional deficiencies
  • Greater risk of osteoporosis
  • Anxiety and depression
  • Growth issues in adolescents
Severe Underweight (BMI < 16)
  • Significant nutritional deficiencies
  • Major immune system impact
  • Serious bone health issues
  • Increased fertility issues
Normal Weight (BMI 18.5 - 24.9)
  • Low risk for chronic diseases
  • Ideal for maintaining good health
Overweight (BMI 25 - 29.9)
  • Increased risk of cardiovascular diseases
  • Type 2 diabetes, sleep apnea
  • Joint problems, certain cancers
Obesity Class I (BMI 30 - 34.9)
  • Severe cardiovascular issues
  • Metabolic syndrome
  • Psychological effects
Obesity Class II & III (BMI ≥ 35)
  • High risk of heart disease
  • Severe diabetes complications
  • Reduced life expectancy
  • Significant psychological impact
