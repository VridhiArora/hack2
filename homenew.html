Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I work out?

Aim for at least 3–5 times a week, mixing cardio and strength training.

Do I need supplements to build muscle?

Not necessarily; a balanced diet can provide all you need

What’s better: cardio or weights?

Both are important—cardio boosts heart health, weights build strength.

Does working out help with PMS?

Yes, regular physical activity can reduce PMS symptoms over time.

Can I eat carbs and still lose weight?

Yes!! Whole carbs like oats,fruits and veggies are important for energy.

Is skipping meals a good idea?

Not really-skipping meals can slow your metabolism and cause overeating later.
