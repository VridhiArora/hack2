Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I use the BMI Calculator?

To calculate your BMI, simply enter your height (in centimeters or inches) and weight (in kilograms or pounds) in the designated fields. Click the "Calculate" button, and your BMI will be displayed along with an interpretation of the result.

What does my BMI result mean?

Your BMI categorizes you into weight status categories: underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese. This information can help you understand your body composition and guide your health decisions.

How can I track my calories?

To use our calorie tracker, log in to your account and navigate to the "Calorie Tracker" section. You can input your meals and snacks, and the tool will calculate your total calorie intake for the day.

How do I track my water intake?

Access the "Water Intake Tracker" feature on your dashboard. You can log the amount of water you drink throughout the day, and it will help you stay on track with your hydration goals.

Where can I find fitness and diet tips?

Visit our "Fitness" section, where you’ll find a variety of articles and tips on fitness routines, healthy eating, and lifestyle changes that can support your wellness journey.
